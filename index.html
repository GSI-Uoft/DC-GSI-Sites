Mapping Green Stormwater Infrastructure in Washington, DC

Explore green stormwater infrastructure (GSI) sites across Washington, DC. This interactive map supports research into the urban infrastructure that manages stormwater and its relationship to neighborhoods and communities across the city.

Use the map to explore locations and interact with available map layers and site information.

An interactive GIS research project by Isabel Thompson. Map hosted on ArcGIS Online.
